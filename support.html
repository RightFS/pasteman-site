支持与帮助

PasteMan 是 macOS 菜单栏剪贴板历史工具，可在你明确配对的 Mac 之间通过局域网加密同步新内容。

联系支持：szz@joymoe.com

快速使用

打开历史

点击菜单栏中的 PasteMan 图标，或按默认快捷键 Option-V。

复制与粘贴

Enter 尝试粘贴；Command-Enter 仅复制。未授予辅助功能权限时可手动按 Command-V。

设置与暂停

右键或 Control-点击菜单栏图标，可打开设置、暂停记录或同步以及退出应用。

设备同步

在两台 Mac 上打开“设置 → 设备与同步”，启用共享并按六位验证码完成双方配对。

常见问题

  1. 看不到菜单栏图标：检查菜单栏空间，或在活动监视器中退出 PasteMan 后重新打开。
  2. 无法自动粘贴：前往“系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 辅助功能”允许 PasteMan。即使不授权，复制和手动粘贴仍然可用。
  3. 两台 Mac 无法互相发现：确认两台设备在同一普通局域网，PasteMan 已获得“本地网络”权限，且路由器没有启用访客网络隔离。
  4. 没有记录新内容：确认记录未暂停、来源应用未被排除，并检查内容是否被来源应用标记为临时或保密。
  5. 如何删除数据：可在历史中删除单条记录，或在“设置 → 历史与隐私”中清理历史；可信设备可在“设备与同步”中解除。

Support

PasteMan is a macOS menu bar clipboard-history utility that can securely sync new items between Macs you explicitly pair on your local network.

Quick start

Open history

Click the PasteMan menu bar icon or press the default Option-V shortcut.

Copy and paste

Press Enter to attempt paste or Command-Enter to copy only. Manual Command-V works without Accessibility permission.

Settings and pause

Right-click or Control-click the menu bar icon to open Settings, pause recording or sync, or quit.

Device sync

On both Macs, open Settings → Devices & Sync, enable sharing, and complete mutual pairing with the six-digit code.

Troubleshooting

  1. No menu bar icon: check available menu bar space, or quit PasteMan in Activity Monitor and reopen it.
  2. Automatic paste does not work: allow PasteMan under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Copy and manual paste remain available without permission.
  3. Macs cannot discover each other: confirm both are on the same regular local network, Local Network permission is enabled, and guest-network isolation is off.
  4. New items are not recorded: confirm recording is not paused, the source app is not excluded, and the source did not mark the item temporary or confidential.
  5. Delete data: remove individual items from history or clear history under Settings → History & Privacy. Remove trusted devices under Devices & Sync.

Contact

When contacting support, include the PasteMan version, macOS version, Mac chip type, and reproduction steps. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, complete pairing invitations, private keys, or sensitive clipboard content.
